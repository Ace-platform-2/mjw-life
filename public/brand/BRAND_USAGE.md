# MJW Life brand system

Approved on 7 November 2026.

**Primary wordmark:** the user's approved high-gloss metallic ribbon plus the exact words **MJW Life**. Use for the main header, authentication, marketing and branded exports.

**Standalone brand symbol:** the matching 3D teal/blue ribbon. Use for icons, favicon, splash, Today/home navigation, app launcher, and recognisable social surfaces.

**Brand constants:** preserve the ribbon shape, gradient, depth and glow. Do not recreate it as flat geometry, rotate it, or add extra words or taglines. Prefer dark navy #0c1220 behind the marks; use transparent assets for large wordmarks and ribbon-only presentation. Ensure adequate clear space and legibility on mobile.

**Canonical asset mapping:** /brand/mjw-life.webp = primary wordmark; /brand/mjw-ribbon.png = standalone ribbon mark for splash and Today navigation; /brand/mjw-app-192.png and /brand/mjw-app-512.png = ribbon-only installed app icons; /brand/mjw-favicon-64.png = browser favicon; /brand/mjw-apple-touch-180.png = Apple launcher icon; /manifest.webmanifest = installed app identity.

**Asset source of truth:** 3D ribbon and MJW Life wordmark supplied and approved by the MJW Life owner on 7 November 2026. Optimised PNG/WebP derivatives of the approved artwork are now committed under `public/brand/`; do not replace them with an unrelated or flat mark.

**Trademark status:** the ribbon serves as the platform's brand identifier and may be used with trademark notices where appropriate, but no claim of registered trademark status should be made unless the mark has been cleared and registered with the relevant intellectual property authority.
